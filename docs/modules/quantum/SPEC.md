# Quantum Module — SPEC
_Status: PLANNED (Immersion Audit Updated)_

## 1. Learning Objectives (Tier-Adapted)
- **Foundation:** Understand that quantum particles can hold blends of states (superposition) until measured; measurement forces a single outcome; entanglement creates instantaneous correlations; quantum computers are not "magic," but specialized physical systems.
- **Advanced:** Represent qubit states on the Bloch sphere ($|\psi\rangle = \cos\frac{\theta}{2}|0\rangle + e^{i\phi}\sin\frac{\theta}{2}|1\rangle$); explain Bell states ($|\Phi^+\rangle$); differentiate the 7 physical quantum architectures (superconducting transmon, trapped ion, photonic, neutral atom, silicon spin, topological, quantum annealer); analyze Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum (NISQ) limits.
- **Expert:** Formulate density matrices ($\rho$) and open-system Lindblad decoherence ($\dot{\rho}$); trace circuit unitary operations ($U$) for Deutsch-Jozsa, Grover search, and Shor factoring; evaluate surface-code error syndrome extraction and fault-tolerant thresholds ($p_{th} \sim 1\%$).

## 3. Beat Timeline (15–20 minutes)
1. **0:00–2:00 (Beat 1 — Opening Hook):** Stand in a pitch-black void. A single glowing sphere of light hovers before your chest—a superposition state. Sound of deep quantum resonance.
2. **2:00–5:00 (Beat 2 — Bloch Sphere & Measurement):** Rotate the Bloch sphere state vector directly with your hands. Perform measurements; feel state collapse.
3. **5:00–8:00 (Beat 3 — First Physical Challenge):** *Superposition Tuning:* rotate state vector to equal superposition $(|0\rangle + |1\rangle)/\sqrt{2}$ with $\pm 3^\circ$ accuracy.
4. **8:00–12:00 (Beat 4 — The 7 Hardware Architectures Walk-Through):** Walk through 7 room-scale walk-through physical installations (superconducting cryostat, trapped-ion vacuum chamber, photonic table, neutral-atom optical tweezers, silicon quantum dot, topological braided nanowire, D-Wave annealer). Pull exploded views.
5. **12:00–16:00 (Beat 5 — Capstone Challenge: Deutsch-Jozsa & Grover Circuit):** Physically plug quantum logic gates (Hadamard, CNOT, Phase Flip) into physical waveguide tracks to determine oracle function in a single evaluation.
6. **16:00–18:00 (Beat 6 — NISQ Reality & Decoherence):** Watch environmental thermal noise physically decay a coherent state into classical randomness; observe error syndrome measurement.
7. **18:00–20:00 (Beat 7 — Reflection & Exit Horizon):** Earn "Quantum Architect" badge. Review African quantum research (QLA Kigali). Return to Hub.
