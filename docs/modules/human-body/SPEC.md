## 1. Learning Objectives (Tier-Adapted)
- **Foundation:** Understand how the heart pumps blood; how electrical impulses trigger brain thoughts and muscle reflexes; how cancer results from uncontrolled cell division; how HIV targets CD4+ immune cells and how modern ART treatment halts viral spread (U=U).
- **Advanced:** Detail the cardiac conduction cycle (SA node $\to$ AV node $\to$ Purkinje fibers $\to$ Wiggers diagram); trace spinal reflex arcs and action potential ion fluxes ($Na^+/K^+$); analyze the 6 biological Hallmarks of Cancer and CAR-T cell immunotherapy; explain HIV life cycle enzymes (Reverse Transcriptase, Integrase, Protease) and cellular mucosal immunity findings from the Pumwani Cohort.
- **Expert:** Formulate ventricular Frank-Starling hemodynamics and pressure-volume loops; calculate Hodgkin-Huxley membrane currents; evaluate oncogenic signaling cascades (KRAS, p53, PD-1/PD-L1 checkpoint blockade); analyze cytotoxic T-lymphocyte (CTL) epitope recognition kinetics and HLA-restricted resistance mechanisms.
