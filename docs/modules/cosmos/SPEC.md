## 1. Learning Objectives (Tier-Adapted)
- **Foundation:** Understand that gravity keeps planets in orbit; the Karman line is an arbitrary human boundary; light takes 8+ minutes to reach Earth from the Sun; black holes are gravitational bodies, not vacuum cleaners.
- **Advanced:** Calculate orbital velocities ($v_o = \sqrt{GM/r}$); explain atmospheric scale height ($H \approx 8.5\text{ km}$); differentiate stellar life-cycle endpoints (white dwarf vs neutron star vs black hole) by Chandrasekhar and Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoff limits; interpret black hole anatomy (Schwarzschild radius $r_s = 2GM/c^2$, photon sphere, accretion disc Doppler beaming).
- **Expert:** Derive the vis-viva equation; formulate relativistic aberration and Doppler factor $D = \frac{1}{\gamma(1 - \beta \cos\theta)}$ for high-velocity travel; explain why faster-than-light travel violates causality in Special Relativity; evaluate supermassive black hole spin parameters using the Kerr metric.

## 4. Diegetic Spatial Anchors & Micro-Simulations

### Atmosphere Layers & Karman Line
- **Level 1 Spatial Tag:** "The Karman line (~100 km) is an internationally recognized convention where atmosphere becomes too thin for aerodynamic lift, not a physical brick wall."
- **Level 2 3D Micro-Simulation:**
  - *Foundation:* Scrubbable air-density gauge; moving a hand through the air shows molecule density decreasing from thick sea-level fog to empty vacuum.
  - *Advanced:* Interactive barometric scale slider showing exponential pressure drop $P = P_0 e^{-h/H}$ with live barometric readout.
  - *Expert:* Dynamic drag vs lift vector visualizer demonstrating where orbital velocity exceeds aerofoil stall speed.

### Sagittarius A* Black Hole
- **Level 1 Spatial Tag:** "A supermassive black hole containing 4 million times the mass of our Sun. Its gravity is so intense that nothing, not even light, can escape within its event horizon."
- **Level 2 3D Micro-Simulation:**
  - *Foundation:* Grab and toss an illuminated probe toward the horizon; watch it appear to freeze and redden due to gravitational redshift.
  - *Advanced:* Interactive slicing plane cutting through the photon sphere ($1.5 r_s$) and innermost stable circular orbit (ISCO).
  - *Expert:* Relativistic ray-marching visualization demonstrating frame-dragging (Lense-Thirring effect) and Kerr metric event horizon spin parameters.
