## Unverified Claims & Explicit Labels
- **Exoplanet Surface Textures:** All surface visuals for Proxima b, TRAPPIST-1e, 51 Pegasi b, Kepler-16b, 55 Cancri e, and Kepler-452b are strictly labeled **"Artist's conception — direct surface resolution is impossible with current optical instruments"**.
- **Proxima b Atmosphere:** Atmosphere composition remains unverified by JWST transmission spectroscopy to date; labeled **"Atmospheric status: Unknown"**.
- **Warp Drive:** Faster-than-light travel is strictly presented under **"Cinematic Warp (Science Fiction)"** with Dr. Amani calling out that superluminal propagation violates Lorentz covariance and causality.
