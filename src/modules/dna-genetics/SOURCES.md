## Claim handling notes

- The earlier draft carried an “up to 90 % protection” figure from the research notes. It is **not** used in learner copy: the exact numeric estimate depends on the study population and outcome definition, and the cited Williams 2005 paper records association rather than a single universal percentage. The exhibit says “strong protection against severe malaria” instead. If a reviewer wants the numeric claim reinstated, a population-specific source must be added first.
- The red blood cell model is a **simplified analogy**. Real red cells are biconcave discs; the crescent shape represents the sickling tendency in HbSS, not a diagnosis for any individual.
- CRISPR material is a non-clinical targeting model. It does not provide guide design, dosing, laboratory protocols, or treatment instructions.
- Every active simulation is labelled “Simplified model — not engineering grade.”

## Medical and scientific limits

- Sickle-cell material is educational, respectful, non-diagnostic, and does not predict any individual's health.
- Trait (HbAS) and disease (HbSS) are kept explicitly distinct in the exhibit and the narrative.
- Agricultural genomics is presented as institutional research context, not as a product claim about any specific crop.
